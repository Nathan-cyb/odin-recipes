Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made by layering sheets of pasta with a rich sauce, cheese, and various fillings. A traditional meat lasagna typically includes a tomato-based sauce made with ground beef, onions, garlic, and herbs such as oregano and basil. The dish is assembled by alternating layers of cooked pasta, meat sauce, ricotta or béchamel sauce, and mozzarella cheese, then topped with Parmesan cheese for extra flavor

Once assembled, the lasagna is baked in the oven until the cheese is melted and golden brown. After baking, it is usually left to rest for several minutes before serving, which helps the layers hold together when sliced. The combination of tender pasta, savory sauce, and creamy cheese creates a hearty and satisfying meal that is enjoyed in many variations, including vegetarian and seafood versions

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna pasta sheets
  • 500g (1 lb)ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2-3 garlic cloves, mined
  • 800g (28oz)crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon of dried basil
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Black pepper (to taste)
  • 425g (15oz)ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat a pan and cook the chopped onion until soft. Add the garlic and ground beef, then cook until the meat is browned.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer the sauce for 20-30 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese and egg until well combined.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
  6. Add a layer of lasagna noodles over the sauce.
  7. Spread some of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, then add meat sauce and a layer of mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers of noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella until all ingredients are used.
  9. Finish with a final layer of meat sauce and top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Cover the dish with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes.
  11. Remove the foil and bake for another 20-25 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  12. Let the lasagna rest for 10-15 minutes before slicing and serving.
